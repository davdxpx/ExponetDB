# Avatar and separator

Two small, passive components. `Avatar` (with `AvatarImage` and `AvatarFallback`) marks a person
or a client with a picture or their initials. `Separator` draws a hairline between pieces of
content, horizontal or vertical. Neither is interactive and neither may be the only carrier of
information: the name is always in the text next to the avatar, and a separator never replaces a
heading or spacing.

## When to use

- Use an **avatar** next to a name in a list, a header or an audit event, to help people scan
  for the same actor. Use initials; a picture that comes from a client or a person's
  own upload is untrusted content.
- Use a **separator** to group related content inside one container, where a heading would be too
  much: between the fields of a popover, between the parts of a header line.
- Do not use an avatar as a button. Put a button around it with a name. Do not stack avatars
  to suggest a team unless the count is also written.
- Do not use a separator for spacing, and do not use it where the structure is already clear.

## Anatomy

- **Avatar:** a 40 px circle (override with `size-*`). `AvatarImage` shows a picture and
  `AvatarFallback` shows when there is none, or while it loads or after it fails. In the Web App
  use the fallback with initials.
- **Initials:** one letter from each of up to two words, capitalized. Derive them from the
  visible letters of the name: strip invisible and direction characters first, and take the
  first character by code point, so a hidden character or an emoji cluster is never half taken.
- **Separator:** a one pixel line in the muted hairline color, full width when horizontal and full
  height when vertical (give a vertical one a height by its container). It is decorative by default
  (`decorative`), which hides it from assistive technology.

## States

- **Avatar with an image:** the image when it loads, the fallback otherwise. An image may only come
  from the app's own origin; never load a picture from a URL supplied by a client.
- **Fallback:** the initials on a quiet surface with a border.
- **Separator:** one look. Set `decorative={false}` only when the line is a real boundary that a
  screen reader should announce.

## Accessibility

- An avatar is decorative when the name is next to it (`aria-hidden` on the fallback, as in the
  demo). When it stands alone, give it `role="img"` and the person's name as its label.
- Never rely on the avatar colors or initials to tell people apart; two people can share initials.
- A separator is hidden from assistive technology by default. Use headings and lists for
  structure instead.
- Contrast: initials hold 4.5:1 on the fallback surface.

## Untrusted content

Names that produce initials come from outside. Strip invisible and direction characters before
taking letters, and show the full name next to the avatar with `UntrustedName` so a person can see
what the initials stand for.

## Copy

- Initials are uppercase letters, at most two. Do not use a punctuation mark or an emoji as an
  initial.
- The label of a stand-alone avatar is the person's name, not "Avatar" or "Profile picture".
