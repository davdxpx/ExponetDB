# Radio group

A radio group shows every option at once and holds exactly one choice. This module
has four styles on one Radix radio group: the plain `RadioGroup` with
`RadioGroupItem`, and three bigger item styles for options that need room,
`RadioGroupLargeItem`, `RadioGroupCard` with `RadioGroupCardItem`, and
`RadioGroupStacked` with `RadioGroupStackedItem`.

## When to use

- Use radios when there are **two to four** options, the person should compare them,
  and one must be chosen: the scope of a backup, the timing of an apply, the class of
  a plan.
- Use a [Select](/ui/select) for a longer list, a [Toggle group](/ui/toggle-group)
  for a view mode that applies at once, and a [Switch](/ui/checkbox-and-switch) for a
  yes or no.
- Prefer the plain style inside forms. Use the larger styles when each option has a
  consequence the person must read before choosing, and put the consequence in the
  description:
  - **Large item:** a card-sized radio with a short label and optional content above it.
  - **Card item:** the same with a label that can be rich (a node) and an icon or a
    picture above it.
  - **Stacked item:** full-width rows with a label and a description under it, for
    options that need a sentence to explain.
- Preselect the safe option. A group in which every option has a consequence starts
  empty and is required.

## Anatomy

- The **group**: a `div` with `role="radiogroup"` and a name (`aria-label` or
  `aria-labelledby` pointing at a heading or legend).
- An **item**: a round indicator and a label. In the three larger styles the whole
  card or row is the radio and the label is its accessible name.
- A **description** (stacked style) is read after the label through `aria-describedby`.

## States

- **Unchecked and checked.** The checked item has a filled indicator and, in the large
  styles, a stronger border and fill.
- **Hover and focus.** The border strengthens on hover and the focus ring shows on
  keyboard focus.
- **Disabled** (the group or one item): half opacity, not selectable. Say why a disabled
  option is unavailable in its description.
- **Invalid:** the group is required and nothing is chosen. The message is shown below
  the group, tied to it with `aria-describedby`.

## Accessibility

- Name the group. Without a name a screen reader announces only "radio button".
- Arrow keys move the choice between items (Up and Left go back, Down and Right go
  forward, wrapping); Tab enters and leaves the group at the chosen item. Do not add
  your own arrow-key handling.
- In the plain style, tie each label to its item with `htmlFor` and `id`. The larger
  styles name themselves from their label; give a card item a plain-text `label`, or
  a node whose text is the name.
- Color is not the only sign of the choice: the indicator and the border differ.

## Copy

- Labels are short and parallel in form: "This target", "The whole cluster".
- A description says what happens, not what the option is called again: "Waits for
  the next window of the cluster."
- Put the safest option first, and write the consequence of the risky one.
