# Resizable

`ResizablePanelGroup`, `ResizablePanel` and `ResizableHandle` split an area into panels
whose sizes the person can change. They are the Web App's split views: the section menu
beside a page, a list beside a detail, a result beside its editor. The module also
exports the handle hooks of the underlying library: `usePanelRef` and `useGroupRef`
(to collapse, expand or read a panel) and `useResizableLayout` (to save a layout).

## When to use

- Use it for a layout where two or three regions share a fixed area and different people
  want different splits: a master and a detail, a code editor and its result.
- Do not use it for page columns that never change (use a grid), for a menu that
  collapses (use the [sidebar](/ui/sidebar)), or on small screens, where stacked or
  separate views work better than a tiny pane.
- Do not nest more than two levels. Give every panel a sensible `minSize`, so no region
  can be dragged to nothing by accident.

## Anatomy

- The **group** sets the direction (`orientation="horizontal"` or `"vertical"`) and fills
  its container; give the container a height.
- **Panels** carry an `id`, a `defaultSize` and limits. A **number is pixels and a string is a
  percentage**: `defaultSize="35%"`, `minSize={240}`. Add `collapsible` and `collapsedSize` to
  let a panel shrink away.
- A **handle** sits between two panels. It is one pixel wide (with a larger hit area) and
  shows a grip when `withHandle` is set.
- The group lays its panels out itself, with inline styles written through the CSSOM.

## States

- **Resting, hovered, active (dragging), focused.** The handle shows the grip on hover,
  focus and drag, and its line darkens while dragged.
- **At a limit.** The handle stops at the neighbor's `minSize` or `maxSize`.
- **Collapsed.** A collapsible panel shrinks to `collapsedSize` when it is dragged past its
  minimum, or when code calls `collapse()` on its ref; `expand()` restores it.
- **Saved layout.** The module saves nothing by default. The app decides whether to remember
  a layout: pass `defaultLayout` and save from `onLayoutChanged`, or use
  `useResizableLayout` with an `id` and a storage the app names. Layouts are per person and
  per browser, never in a URL.

## Accessibility

- A handle is a focusable `separator` that exposes its position (`aria-valuenow`). Left and
  Right (or Up and Down in a vertical group) move it, Home and End go to the limits.
- Give each handle a specific `aria-label` when there is more than one in the layout ("Resize
  the schema list"). The default is "Resize panels".
- Everything a drag can do must be possible by keyboard, and panel content must not depend
  on a minimum width that the handle can take away.
- Collapsing a panel hides its content from the tab order; provide a visible control to
  bring it back.

## CSP notes

The library changes the page cursor while a handle is dragged and sets panel sizes with
inline styles. Both go through CSSOM writes and constructable stylesheets, which
`style-src 'self'` allows; nothing creates a `style` element.

## Copy

- Handle labels name what changes size: "Resize the schema list".
- Panel headings are nouns ("Schema", "Definition"). The names of collapse and expand buttons
  say which panel: "Collapse the list".
