# Tabs

Tabs divide one view into panels the person switches between, with only one panel
visible at a time. The parts are `Tabs`, `TabsList`, `TabsTrigger`, `TabsContent` and
`TabsIndicator` (the underline that slides under the active tab). The sliding indicator
is optional: without it each trigger draws its own underline.

## When to use

- Use tabs for **parts of one thing** that the person looks at one at a time: the
  summary, steps and evidence of a plan; the sections of a settings panel.
- Use a [navigation menu](/ui/nav-menu) when each choice is a **page with its own
  address**. The sections of a target (Schema, Migrations, SQL, Performance) are
  routes, so they are links, not tabs. If a choice should be linkable, bookmarkable or
  shared, it is not a tab.
- Use a [Toggle group](/ui/toggle-group) for a view mode or a range that changes how the
  same content is shown.
- Do not use tabs for steps of a process that must be done in order, for more than about
  six choices, or for a choice that hides content a person must see to proceed.

## Anatomy

- The **list** (`TabsList`): a row of triggers with a bottom border. Give it an
  `aria-label` that names the set of tabs ("Plan sections").
- The **trigger** (`TabsTrigger`): a text label, optionally with a count. The active one
  is in the foreground color with an underline.
- The **panel** (`TabsContent`): the content of one tab, 16 px below the list. It is
  focusable so a keyboard user can reach scrollable content.
- The **indicator** (`TabsIndicator`): a one pixel line that slides to the active tab.
  Put it last inside the list. It follows the active tab by two custom properties on the
  list that the `useTabIndicator` hook measures and writes.

## States

- **Active and inactive.** Selected by click, or by the arrow keys when focused
  (`activationMode` is automatic by default; use `manual` when showing a panel is
  expensive, so the arrows only move focus and Enter or Space select).
- **Disabled.** A disabled tab is skipped by the keyboard. Say elsewhere why it is
  unavailable.
- **Focus.** The trigger shows an inset outline (the tab list is flush with its panel,
  so a ring outside would be clipped).
- **Counts** in a label are text: "Plans (3)". A count of a read that failed is not
  zero; show a failed state in the panel and no count in the label.

## Accessibility

- Tabs follow the WAI-ARIA tab pattern: Tab enters the tab list at the active trigger,
  arrow keys move between triggers, Home and End jump, Tab again moves into the panel.
  Do not add other handlers.
- The list is a `tablist` and needs a name; triggers are `tab`s with `aria-selected`;
  panels are `tabpanel`s labelled by their trigger.
- Do not unmount panels carelessly: a form in a hidden tab loses its state if the panel is
  removed. Keep state in the parent or keep the panel mounted.
- The indicator is decorative and hidden from assistive technology.

## CSP notes

The indicator's position is written with `style.setProperty` (CSSOM) on the tab list as
`--active-tab-left` and `--active-tab-width`, and the slide is a CSS transition on them
(`useTabIndicator`). No `style` element is created. The slide does not run for people who
ask for reduced motion.

## Copy

- Labels are one or two nouns: "Summary", "Steps", "Evidence". No verbs and no
  trailing punctuation.
- Keep labels parallel in form and short enough to fit on one line on a phone; if they do
  not fit, the set is too large for tabs.
- A count follows the label in parentheses, or is omitted when it adds nothing.
