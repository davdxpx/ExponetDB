# Calendar

`Calendar` is a month grid for choosing a day or a range of days. It is
`react-day-picker` with the system's styles, so every option of that library (modes,
`disabled` matchers, `numberOfMonths`, `captionLayout`, locales) works. The Web App uses it
inside a popover as a date picker (see the date picker pattern in
`@exponetdb/ui-patterns`) and for maintenance windows and time ranges.

<Demo id="ui/calendar/single" />

## When to use

- Use it to choose a **day** when the neighbors matter: a window in the next two weeks, a
  start and an end.
- Use a text input with a format hint for an exact date or a timestamp that people copy
  from elsewhere, and a [Select](/ui/select) for a few fixed ranges ("Last 24 hours").
  A calendar is slow for dates far from today.
- Do not use it for times of day; pair it with a time input. Say the time zone next to any
  date that becomes a time.

## Anatomy

- A **caption** with the month and year and two buttons (previous and next month).
- A **weekday row** and a **grid** of days. Days outside the month are shown dimmed
  (`showOutsideDays`) or hidden.
- Modifiers have their own look: **today** (a neutral fill), **selected** and **range** (the
  spot color tint), **disabled** (dimmed) and **outside** (dimmed).
- The component is plain `react-day-picker`: pass `classNames` to change a part; the
  component merges them with its own.

<Demo id="ui/calendar/range" />

## States

- **Single:** one day selected, or none. **Range:** a start, a start and an end, with the
  days between tinted.
- **Disabled days** are not selectable and are skipped by the keyboard. Say why they are
  disabled nearby.
- **Today** is marked even when it is not selected. Pass `today` in a test or a demo so it
  is deterministic.
- **Month navigation** does not change the selection.

## Accessibility

- The grid is a labelled `grid` ("October 2026") of day buttons with full names ("October
  14th, 2026"). Arrow keys move by day and week, Page Up and Page Down by month, Home and
  End to the start and end of the week, Enter or Space select.
- The month label is announced when it changes (a polite status).
- Selected, today and disabled states are exposed (`aria-selected`, `aria-disabled`), not only
  colored.
- Do not make a date picker the only way to enter a date; a typed field is faster for
  keyboard users and for dates far away.
- Weekday and month names follow the locale. The Web App's strings are English (OD-1), so
  keep the locale of the calendar consistent with the page.

## Copy

- Label the control that opens the calendar with the thing it sets ("Maintenance window
  starts"), and write the chosen date in full in the trigger ("October 20, 2026").
- Say what limits the choice: "Choose a day from today on."
- Show the time zone beside any value that carries a time ("UTC").
