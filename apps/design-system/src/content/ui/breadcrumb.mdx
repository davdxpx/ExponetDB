# Breadcrumb

A breadcrumb shows where the current page sits in a hierarchy and lets people go up to
any ancestor: Infrastructure, then a cluster, then Roles. The parts are `Breadcrumb` (the
landmark), `BreadcrumbList`, `BreadcrumbItem`, `BreadcrumbLink`, `BreadcrumbPage`,
`BreadcrumbSeparator` and `BreadcrumbEllipsis`.

<Demo id="ui/breadcrumb/trail" />

## When to use

- Use it on pages that are several levels deep and whose parents are pages of their own:
  a cluster's roles, a plan inside a target, a node's jobs.
- Do not use it as the only navigation, for a flat app, or for steps of a process (that is a
  stepper). It shows where you are, not the history of where you have been.
- The target header chain (`Product / Project / Environment` with its menus) is not a
  breadcrumb. It is a switcher, with its own pattern and tier chip.

## Anatomy

- A `nav` named "Breadcrumb" holding an ordered list. Ancestors are **links**
  (`BreadcrumbLink`, often `asChild` around the app's link), the last item is the
  **current page** (`BreadcrumbPage`, not a link, `aria-current="page"`).
- **Separators** are chevrons between items; they are decorative and hidden from assistive
  technology.
- **Ellipsis** (`BreadcrumbEllipsis`) stands for hidden middle levels on a long trail. It is only a
  glyph with the text "More" for screen readers. If people must reach the hidden levels, put it
  inside a menu trigger button with a name ("Show hidden levels"), otherwise the levels are
  unreachable.
- The list wraps on narrow screens rather than overflowing.

## States

- **Link:** underlined on small screens and quiet on large ones, darker on hover, with the focus
  ring.
- **Current page:** foreground color, not a link.
- **Loading or unknown level.** A level whose name is still loading shows a skeleton of the
  name's width; a level whose read failed shows its path segment as text, not an empty item.

## Untrusted content

Names in a trail come from the registry or the database. Show them with `UntrustedName` (as in
the demo below), which makes invisible and direction characters visible and cuts long
values, and never build a link target from a name without encoding it.

## Accessibility

- The landmark is named "Breadcrumb"; if a page has two trails, give each a distinct name.
- The current page carries `aria-current="page"` and is not a link. Do not repeat the page title
  as a link.
- Do not rely on the chevrons: the list order and the link text carry the meaning.
- Links need a focus ring and enough space to be targets of at least 24 by 24 pixels.

## Copy

- Items repeat the page titles of the levels ("Infrastructure", "pg-main", "Roles"); object
  names appear as stored.
- Do not add "Home" to every trail. The first item is the section.
- Keep items short; truncate long names with the full value available.
