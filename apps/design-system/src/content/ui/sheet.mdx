# Sheet

`Sheet` is a [dialog](/ui/dialog) that slides in from an edge of the screen. It is
modal in the same way: focus is trapped and restored, the page behind is inert and its
scrolling is locked. Choose it when the work needs more room than a dialog gives but is
still a side trip from the page: a form with several fields, an editor, the details of
an operation. The parts are `Sheet`, `SheetTrigger`, `SheetContent`, `SheetHeader`,
`SheetTitle`, `SheetDescription`, `SheetSection`, `SheetFooter` and `SheetClose`.

## When to use

- Use a sheet for a form of more than about three fields, a detail view that the person
  reads next to the page, or an editor that opens from a row.
- Use a [Dialog](/ui/dialog) for a short task, an [Alert dialog](/ui/alert-dialog) to
  confirm, and a page when the content needs its own address, to be linked or to be
  compared with the page behind.
- A sheet opens from the **right** unless there is a strong reason: the left edge is
  the navigation, and the top and bottom edges are for short messages on small screens.
- Do not nest sheets. A sheet may open the discard confirmation and nothing else.

## Anatomy

- **Overlay:** a dimmed, blurred layer. A sheet may be created without one
  (`hasOverlay={false}`) for a panel that must not dim the page; it is still modal.
- **Content:** a panel fixed to one `side` (`right`, `left`, `top` or `bottom`) on the
  popover surface with a border on the inner edge.
- **Header:** `SheetTitle` (`text-lg`, the accessible name) and `SheetDescription`.
- **Sections:** `SheetSection` blocks of content, each padded. Make the section area
  scroll (`overflow-y-auto`) and keep the header and the footer fixed.
- **Footer:** `SheetFooter` with the actions, the main one last. On a phone the buttons
  stack with the main one on top.
- **Close button:** `X` at the top right, named "Close". `showClose={false}` removes it.
- **Size:** `size` sets the width of a side sheet (or the height of a top or bottom
  one): `content`, `default` (a third of the screen from `lg` up), `sm` (a quarter),
  `lg` (a half), `xl`, `xxl`, `full`, and the fixed `w656` and `w800` (656 and 800 px
  from `sm` up). Below `sm` the fixed widths fill the screen. Prefer the fixed widths for
  forms so that a field has the same width on every screen.

## States

- **Closed and open.** Use `open` and `onOpenChange` to control it.
- **Dirty.** The same guard as the dialog: `onRequestClose` runs for Escape, a click on
  the overlay, the close button and any `SheetClose`. Return `false` and ask with the
  in-app discard confirmation. See [Modality and dismissal](/guidance/modality) for the
  complete flow and the dirty-guard pattern.
- **Pending.** While the footer action runs, show it loading and keep the sheet open;
  close it when the action succeeds.

## Accessibility

- Every `SheetContent` has a `SheetTitle` and a `SheetDescription`.
- It is a modal dialog: Tab cycles inside, Escape closes, focus returns to the trigger.
- The close button's name is "Close". Do not remove the footer's Cancel because a close
  button exists; both are expected.
- A sheet is not a navigation drawer. The sidebar becomes a sheet on small screens and
  names itself "Navigation".
- Keep reading order the same as visual order: header, sections, footer.

## CSP notes

A sheet is a modal Radix dialog. Its scroll lock uses the constructable-stylesheet
replacement described on [Dialog](/ui/dialog), so no `style` element is created under
`style-src 'self'`.

## Copy

- The title repeats the action that opened the sheet: "Register a target" after a
  "Register target" button. The main button creates the plan: "Create plan".
- The description says what the sheet does and what it does not do ("It does not create
  the database").
- The dismissing button is "Cancel"; with unsaved changes the confirmation is "Discard
  changes?" with "Keep editing".
