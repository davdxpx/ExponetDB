# Spacing and radius

Space and shape are not tokens of their own in `@exponetdb/tokens`; they are
Tailwind's scales plus a few named steps, used the same way everywhere so that a
button, an input and a select line up without anyone measuring.

## Spacing

Spacing is Tailwind's 4 px grid: `1` is 4 px, `2` is 8 px, `4` is 16 px. Use
steps from the grid and do not invent values with brackets (`p-[13px]`).

- **Inside a control:** the control sizes below set their own padding.
- **Between related items:** `gap-2` (8 px) for a row of buttons or chips,
  `gap-1.5` between a label and its control.
- **Between fields of a form:** `gap-4` to `gap-7`; `FieldSet` uses `gap-6` and
  `FieldGroup` uses `gap-7`.
- **Panels:** cards use `px-(--card-padding-x)`, which is 16 px (`--padding-x-sm`);
  `--padding-x-md` is 24 px. The named step `p-card` reads the same variable.
  Dialog bodies use `px-4 md:px-5` and `py-4`, sheets `px-5`.
- **Between sections of a page:** at least `gap-6`. Use one gap per level and do
  not stack margins from both sides.

## Control heights

Buttons, inputs, select triggers and combobox triggers share four heights, so
they align in a row: **tiny** 26 px, **small** 34 px, **medium** 38 px and
**large** 42 px. Pass the same `size` to controls that sit together.

- `Button` defaults to `tiny`; `Input`, `Textarea` and select triggers default to
  `small`. Next to an input, set the button's `size` explicitly.
- An icon-only button is square at the height of its size (26, 34, 38 or 42 px).
- The small and medium sizes are 16 px text below the `md` breakpoint and 14 px
  from it; tiny is always 12 px.

## Radius

Corners are Tailwind's radius scale plus two named steps.

- `rounded-md` (6 px) is the default of buttons, inputs, menus, tooltips and
  popovers. The larger control sizes scale their radius a little with the height.
- `rounded-lg` (8 px) is cards, alerts and dialogs. `rounded-xl` (12 px) is an inset
  layout surface.
- `rounded-sm` (4 px) is checkboxes and small chips; `rounded-xs` (2 px) is the
  close button of an overlay.
- `rounded-item` (4 px, `rounded-md` minus 2 px) is an item inside a rounded menu, so
  its highlight nests inside the menu's own corner. `rounded-panel` is 6 px.
- `rounded-full` is for pills, badges, switches, avatars and sliders.

Nest radii: the inner radius is the outer radius minus the padding, never larger.

## Breakpoints

Tailwind's defaults (`sm` 640 px, `md` 768 px, `lg` 1024 px, `xl` 1280 px, `2xl`
1536 px) plus `xs` at 480 px. Design for a phone first and add rules upward.
`useIsMobile` reports true below 768 px, which is where `Sidebar` turns into a
sheet. Page content has a maximum width by purpose, from `PAGE_SIZE_CLASSES`:
**small** 768 px (`max-w-3xl`), **medium** and the default 1024 px (`max-w-5xl`),
**large** 1280 px (`max-w-7xl`), each with `px-8`, and **full** at `w-full`.

## Hit areas

A small control may have a pointer target bigger than its box. The `hit-area-<n>`
utility adds an invisible area of `n` steps on every side without changing the
layout (`hit-area-x-<n>`, `-y-`, `-l-`, `-r-`, `-t-` and `-b-` grow one axis or
side). Icon-only buttons use `hit-area-1`; the close buttons of dialogs and sheets use
`hit-area-6`. `hit-area-debug` draws the area while you work and must not ship.

## Rules

- A target is at least 24 by 24 px, including its hit area (WCAG 2.2, 2.5.8). The
  smallest control, an icon-only tiny button, is 26 by 26 px.
- Two neighboring targets do not share a hit area.
- Containers set a `min-w-0` where text must truncate inside a flex or grid row;
  otherwise the text pushes the row wider than its parent.
- Do not size a text container in pixels; use `max-w-*` in `rem` so zoom works.
- Do not reach into a component's padding from outside. If a layout needs a different
  gap, change the gap of the container.

## Accessibility

- Spacing is what makes focus rings, tooltips and error text readable. A control
  keeps room for its ring (2 px plus a 2 px offset) so a neighbor does not cover it.
- Reflow: at 320 px wide a page scrolls vertically only. Rows wrap (`flex-wrap`);
  tables scroll inside their own region (see [Table](/ui/table)).

## Copy

Nothing in this page is user-facing text. Keep labels short enough for the
smallest size of the control they sit in, and truncate with a way to see the rest
instead of wrapping a label into two lines inside a control.
