# Focus

Keyboard focus is always visible. Two utilities from `@exponetdb/tokens` draw it, a
single token colors it, and every interactive primitive in `@exponetdb/ui` already
applies one of them. A new control picks the same utility instead of writing its own
ring.

## The ring color

`--ring` (`ring-ring`, `outline-ring`) is `primary-bright`: an opaque version of the
spot color that holds at least 3:1 against the page and against every surface it is
drawn on (WCAG 1.4.11). It follows the theme and the spot color, and it never
depends on transparency over an unknown background.

## focus-ring

The default. A two pixel ring offset by two pixels from the control, painted only
on `:focus-visible`, so a mouse click does not show it and the keyboard always does.
The utility also sets `outline-hidden`; the ring replaces the browser outline, it
is never simply removed.

Use `focus-ring` on buttons, inputs, checkboxes, switches, links and most controls.
It is the right choice anywhere there is room around the control for the ring.

## focus-inset

For dense and flush surfaces where a ring outside the box would be clipped or would
collide with a neighbor: interactive table rows, tabs, accordion triggers, the
trigger of a collapsible, a scroll region that can take focus. It draws a two pixel
**outline** inside the box (`outline-offset: -2px`).

- It must be an outline, not a ring or a box shadow. A shadow on a table row is
  unreliable in Safari.
- Do not combine it with `outline-hidden` or `outline-none`; that sets the outline
  style to none and hides it.

## In components

- **Controls** (`Button`, `Input`, `Checkbox`, `Switch`, `Slider`) use `focus-ring`.
  `Input` also moves its border to `border-control-hover` while focused.
- **Groups** whose focus lands on an inner element (`InputGroup`) draw the ring on
  the shell with `:has(:focus-visible)`, so addons are inside the ring.
- **Flush items** (`TabsTrigger`, `AccordionTrigger`, `CollapsibleTrigger`,
  `TableRow` with a handler, a horizontally scrollable table) use `focus-inset`.
- **Overlays** move focus in when they open, keep it inside while they are modal and
  give it back to the control that opened them when they close. See
  [Dialog](/ui/dialog).

## Rules

- **Never remove a focus indicator** (`outline-none`, `outline-0`, `focus:outline-hidden`
  without a ring). If a component needs a different look, use `focus-inset` or the
  ring tokens, not nothing.
- **Use `focus-visible`, not `focus`,** for the ring. The utilities already do.
- **Focus must not be hidden.** A sticky header, a toast region or a floating bar
  must not cover the control that has focus (WCAG 2.4.11). Give scroll targets
  scroll margin and keep fixed bars out of the content's tab order.
- **Order follows the layout.** Do not use positive `tabindex`; fix the DOM order
  instead. `tabIndex={-1}` is for programmatic focus and for controls that are
  taken out of the order on purpose.
- **A disabled control leaves the tab order** unless it must explain itself. A
  control that is unavailable for a reason the person needs to read (a plan that
  cannot be applied) stays focusable with `focusableWhenDisabled` and a tooltip that
  gives the reason. See [Button](/ui/button).
- **Return focus after a change.** When an action removes the control that had focus
  (delete a row, close a panel), move focus to the next sensible element, not to the
  top of the page.

## Accessibility

- Visible focus (2.4.7), focus not obscured (2.4.11) and non-text contrast of the
  indicator (1.4.11) are met by the ring token and the two utilities. Do not
  weaken them with a lighter custom ring.
- Radix primitives manage focus for overlays, menus and tabs: arrow keys move
  inside a menu, Escape closes, and a modal traps Tab. Do not replace that with
  your own handlers.
- A tooltip or hover card opens on keyboard focus as well as on hover, so focus
  also reveals what hover reveals.

## Copy

Nothing visible: the focus ring has no text. The accessible name of the focused
control is what is read aloud, so name controls by their action ("Create plan"), not
by their position ("Button 2").
