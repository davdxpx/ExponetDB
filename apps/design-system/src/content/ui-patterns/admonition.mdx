# Admonition and info tooltip

Two components explain things. They differ in who decides when the explanation is read.

| Component | The explanation is | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| `Admonition` | In the page, always visible. | Something the person must know to decide: a risk, a state, a rule that applies. |
| `InfoTooltip` | Behind a small button, read on request. | A definition or a hint the person can do without. |

If a person needs the text to act correctly, it belongs in the page. A tooltip is not read on a phone, by
many keyboard users or by a person who is in a hurry.

## Admonition

An `Admonition` is a callout inside a page or a dialog. It is built on `Alert` and has a type, an
optional title, a description, free content and actions.

| `type` | Means | Announced |
|---|---|---|
| `default` | A note that applies to what follows. | Politely, as a status. |
| `info` | A fact about where the data comes from or how the page works. | Politely. |
| `success` | Something finished and was checked. | Politely. |
| `warning` | A risk or a condition to be aware of. | Politely. |
| `destructive` | Something that destroys or fails. | At once, as an alert. |

Each type has its own icon shape, so color is never the only signal. The title is plain text; it renders
as a text node and never as Markdown or HTML. The description and children are nodes the caller built.

### Layout and actions

`layout` is `vertical` (actions under the text), `horizontal` (actions at the end of the row) or
`responsive` (vertical until the callout is wide enough). Actions are small buttons or links. Give a
callout at most one action that matters and a quiet way to dismiss.

### Rules

- Use sparingly: one callout in a section is a note; four is noise.
- A `success` callout is only for a state that was verified. Do not use it for "applied": applied is not
  verified.
- Keep text produced from content out of the title. Pass it through `UntrustedName` or `UntrustedText` in
  the description.

## Info tooltip

A small "i" button that explains in a tooltip. The tooltip opens on hover and on keyboard focus, closes
with Escape, and is linked to the button with `aria-describedby`. The button has a name: "More
information" by default, or a `label` that says what it explains ("What a plan class is").

<Demo id="ui-patterns/info-tooltip/default" />

- Put it after a label, using `afterLabel` of a form field or a heading.
- Keep it to one or two sentences. If it needs more, link to a page.
- It needs the app's `TooltipProvider`.

## Accessibility

- An `Admonition` is an `Alert`. The destructive type is `role="alert"` and is announced at once; the other types are
  polite status messages, so they do not interrupt.
- The icon of each type has its own shape and is decorative; the title and the text carry the meaning.
- Actions are real buttons and links, in the reading order after the text.
- `InfoTooltip` is a button with a name. The tooltip opens on focus as well as on hover, closes with Escape and does not trap focus.

## Copy

- A title is a sentence fragment that states the fact: "Plans expire", "This plan drops a table".
- A description says what to do or what it means, not what the interface is.
- No exclamation marks, and no "Note:" prefix. The type already says it.
