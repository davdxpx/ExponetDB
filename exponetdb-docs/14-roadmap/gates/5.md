### Review of Phases 1 and 2 (2026-10-07)

An independent adversarial review of the Phase 1 and 2 diff (`723d7ce..cb70381`)
reproduced each finding with an experiment. Every finding is fixed with a
regression test (`agent/node/internal/agent/review_test.go` and the tests
named below) and the decisions are recorded in
[ADR-0024 §7](../../01-architecture/adr/0024-node-trust-model.md#7-refinements-after-the-wave-5-review-2026-10-07).

| # | Severity | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | high | A gateway could replace a buffered success with a node-signed rejection for the same job (forged or replayed delivery) | one statement per job, written once and never replaced; a re-delivery gets the stored statement or nothing; a delivery rejection is sent once, never stored, and is no evidence about the job (protocol.md §4.2) |
| 2 | high | Clock pushed forward, then compaction, then clock set back: the same envelope ran twice | compaction counts back from the earlier of the host clock and the highest accepted `issuedAt`; the nonce cache forgets only by compaction; compaction daily across reconnects |
| 3 | medium | After renewal the agent kept presenting the old certificate on a busy HTTP/2 connection | every request after renewal uses a new connection pool (`transport.SetCertificate`) |
| 4 | medium | A crash during renewal could lock the node out | the gateway accepts the replaced certificate until the renewed one is first presented (`nodes.served_node`, `nodes.renew`; enrollment.md §4); NULL-safe certificate, redeem-hash and outcome checks in the gateway functions |
| 5 | medium | 65 crashed jobs stopped hello for good | crashed jobs are closed as `interrupted` at start, reported for 7 days, at most the 64 most recent; accepted-only jobs abandoned with `start_unacknowledged` |
| 6 | medium | Forged envelopes filled the result buffer | delivery rejections are never stored |
| 7 | low/medium | A far-future signed `issuedAt` made the agent refuse every later job | `issuedAt` more than 30 s ahead of the agent clock is `not_yet_valid` |
| 8 | low | The 64 MiB journal cap against 90-day retention was not sized | budget measured and documented (about 460 simple jobs a day; architecture.md §4); a full journal refuses new jobs without breaking (`ErrFull`) |
| 9 | low | Cancellation did not respect the effect point by step name | a journaled step named like the effect step passes the effect point; a cancel after it completes `failed` |
| 10 | low | TypeScript dropped a top-level `__proto__` member from signed values | `Object.fromEntries` in `jobSigningValue` and `unsignedCompletion`; tests in `node.golden.test.ts` |
| 11 | low | `log.inspect` since/until compared at millisecond precision in TypeScript | nanosecond comparison (`timestampBefore`), the same cases tested in TypeScript and Go |
| 12 | low | No single-instance lock; `status` could cut the live journal's tail | `flock` on the state directory for `run` and `enroll`; `status` opens the journal read-only |
| 13 | low | The clock rule did not match systemd's `NTPSynchronized` | maximum error below 16 s, `STA_UNSYNC` ignored |
| 14 | low | Completions with more than 256 steps were never deliverable; a completion that could not be built was dropped | the last 256 steps are sent (the journal keeps all); a failed build becomes a minimal `failed` statement; a request the agent's own check refuses is parked |
| 15 | nit | Documentation and code disagreed | `protocol.md` §4.3 start order; `config.update@1` reconciliation `observable`; the enrollment chain may not contain a root (enforced); a step is journaled before progress reports it; the size after a torn tail; `ns1` field binding stated precisely; a test that the issuing CA cannot mint a gateway certificate |
