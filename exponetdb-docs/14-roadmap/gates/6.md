Observed outcomes worth naming: a crash of the helper after its fragment
write (`performed`) ends `needs_attention / applied / error`: the write is
on the host, no statement proves a reload, the value is not in effect, and
the job never runs again. A job whose row names another node, or whose
signature comes from a key the node does not trust, is refused by the
Control Store's guard first and, written past it, by the agent (`node_mismatch`,
`unknown_signing_key`) without a step. A cluster replaced after planning
makes the plan stale at apply (`node` binding: `registry_conflict`).
