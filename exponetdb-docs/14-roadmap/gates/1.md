This record follows the model in
[`acceptance-gates.md` "How gates are recorded"](../acceptance-gates.md#how-gates-are-recorded).
Every item links to its evidence. Item statuses are `evidence` (evidence is
complete and waits for the owner) until the owner accepts them. Nothing is
waived; security items (**S**) cannot be waived.

**History.** Wave 1 was implemented in pull request `davdxpx/ExponetDB#3`,
merged into `main` on 2026-10-05 (merge commit `3181ce3`). G1 was **not**
accepted at that point. The owner's independent review of #3 found contract
problems that must be fixed before the hash contracts are frozen; the
**pre-G1 hardening pass** (§2) fixed them on branch `fix/wave-1-g1-hardening`.
Because G1 was not accepted, `c1` had not been frozen and no value had been
stored anywhere, so `c1` was corrected under its own name rather than replaced
by `c2` ([`versioning.md` §5](../../01-architecture/versioning.md#5-version-identifiers-in-the-contracts)).
