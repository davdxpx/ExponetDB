## Update 2026-10-28 (test campaign fixes)

**[OBSERVED]** The campaign of 2026-10-27 tested this baseline on a real systemd host and found five
defects in the backup and restore path that the local evidence above could not show; the fixes of
2026-10-28 are in [`PRE-WAVE-11-REVIEW.md`](./../PRE-WAVE-11-REVIEW.md#test-campaign-fixes--parallel-session)
(TC-009 to TC-013, TC-026, TC-028). Each is **tested locally and not executed against the real
engines in this session** (no pgBackRest, restic or systemd host was available):

- row 3 (restore drills): `latest` and `older` drills ran `--type=immediate` and always failed (TC-009);
  `amcheck: not_run` failed every drill unless the extension existed everywhere (TC-010, OD-60);
- row 4 (backups are verified): `backup.verify` never detected corruption, the node ignored what
  `pgbackrest verify` reports (TC-011);
- logical backups to a local restic repository could not run under the unit's sandbox (TC-012);
- the offline-drill attestation answered 500 for every request (TC-013), and a 5xx now logs its cause;
- the real-engine proofs (`pglab`, `runbook.lab`) now have a CI job, `backup-lab`; **it has not run**
  (CI has never run on this repository), so the rows that cite them stay "not proven on a real host"
  until it or the manual command ([`test-strategy.md` §9](../test-strategy.md#9-what-bun-run-verify-does-not-run))
  has been run.

Nothing in this record claims `PASS`.
