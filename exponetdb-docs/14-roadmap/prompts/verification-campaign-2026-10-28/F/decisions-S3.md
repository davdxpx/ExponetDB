# Open decisions raised by the upgrade, rollback and Control Store loss rehearsals (tester S3)

1. **S3-D1 (rollback story):** upgrades cannot be rolled back by redeploying the old image (reproduced). Choose: (a) document "roll forward or restore the pre-upgrade dump" and make the dump a numbered step; (b) additionally allow an older binary on a newer schema whose extra bundles are marked additive with a declared minimum release (needs an ADR); (c) keep the exact-match start and automate the dump plus a guided restore.
2. **S3-D2 (keyring loss):** RB-14 cannot be executed (reproduced). Either state that keyring loss without an offline copy means a new installation (and name the custodian, a second copy and a yearly restore test), or build an audited offline `keyring reset-secrets` path (re-submit credentials, re-issue the node CA, re-enroll nodes).
3. **S3-D3 (Control Store RPO):** a daily `pg_dumpall` loses up to a day of approvals, plans, operations and audit, and every target applied since needs its own `ledger reconcile` (production with a step-up and an approval). Accept that, or dump before every release and hourly, or give the Control Store a point-in-time path that C-35 can still restore without ExponetDB.
4. **S3-D4 (plans across a release):** observed: a plan made by the old release applies after the upgrade although the policy version hash changed, because the apply re-evaluates the policy and compares decision and requirements (`accept.ts:198`). Keep that, or bind the exact policy version as the constitution's wording ("bound to ... policy version") says.
5. **S3-D5 (a not-ready old role):** observed: after the schema moves ahead, an old role answers 503 but its worker keeps finishing and claiming operations until redeployed. Stop claiming when the schema is not the build's own, or accept it for additive releases.
6. **S3-D6 (what a restore may re-create):** after a restore, operations that were `awaiting_approval` for work that already happened are still offered for approval (ends `PLAN_STALE`, safe). Cancel them at reconcile time, or leave as is.
