- **S4-D1:** a person with a production binding and a prod-scoped CLI grant can plan and apply a standard, evidence-verified production migration with no approval and no fresh proof when no policy rule fires (observed 202, table created). Confirm that is intended (principle 6 only constrains agents).
- **S4-D2:** OD-9 allows the applying person to approve their own production plan (acknowledged + fresh proof); not exercised by S4. Confirm for production, or require a second person.
- **S4-D3:** environment-scoped people cannot use the CLI at all today (finding "The first-party CLI fails with PERMISSION_DENIED"): fix the CLI, or document that CLI users need a global binding.
- **S4-D4:** should plan lint refuse server-file and untrusted-language functions for every target, or leave that to the execution role (this matters for Supabase-origin schemas whose migrator role is more privileged)?
- **S4-D5:** a same-actor repeat of apply on a consumed plan returns the existing operation (202) without a fresh proof even when the plan requires one; harmless today (no re-execution); decide whether it should be 409.

The node reports host secrets as `{"withheld":"secret"}`. 3,377 authorization requests over 7 classes and 2 tiers: no deny-expected/allow-observed row.
