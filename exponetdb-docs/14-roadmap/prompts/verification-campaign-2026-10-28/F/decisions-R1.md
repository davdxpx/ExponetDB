# Open decisions raised by tester R1 (block R)

### D-R1-1 May `migration.verify` close another operation without approval or step-up? (TC-005)
`migration.verify` is an inline plan of fixed risk `low` (`packages/contracts/src/actions.ts:964`, scope `migrations.apply`, no step-up). Since the TC-005 fix, running it for a migration whose `needs_attention` apply operation holds the target's schema domain closes that operation (`verification_failed`, an `unknown` apply outcome becomes `partially_applied`) and releases the domain, on production too. `migration.resume` (risk `high`) closes in the same way but under its approval and re-authentication. The finding proposed "the same human re-authentication". Options: (a) accept: the person holds `migrations.apply:<tier>` and the audit event names who superseded what; (b) make a verify plan that names a stuck operation `standard` or `high` (approval on production, step-up) when `supersedes` is set; (c) restrict closing to `migration.resume` and a new explicit operator action. Recommendation: (b), because the gate being removed is the one that makes a person look at an operation of unknown outcome. Note the separate defect: the close currently commits even when the recovery is refused (finding "closes the stuck operation before the recovery's own checks").

### D-R1-2 What is "verified" for a repository whose WAL has a hole? (TC-011)
`pgbackrest verify` 2.60 reports `status: ok` for a repository whose archive lost a segment, even the stop segment of a backup (`R/R1-notes/TC-011.md`), and the Control Plane only runs label-scoped verifications. Options: (a) the node computes archive continuity itself (segment listing) and `backup.verify` plus the catalog use it (the finding "backup.verify passes a repository with a deleted WAL segment"); (b) accept pgBackRest's definition and document that continuity is proven only by a `latest` drill; (c) schedule a whole-repository verification plus a `latest` drill as the protection criterion. Recommendation: (a) and (c).

### D-R1-3 Strictness of amcheck (OD-60, recorded by the fix)
The fix made `amcheck` advisory (`not_run` passes, `failed`/`error` fail). Real run: `168 indexes checked in 1 of 3 databases; amcheck is missing in the others` and the drill `passed` with RTO 3 s. This is the "reduced assurance" the finding offered; the owner decides if the production protection state should carry it as a visible reason (it does not today: `reasons` lists `engine_advisory` but not `amcheck_partial`).
