## Rollback anchor

The image tag `sha256-c947c3d2054f3da03975e31c6cf137351fe3a3a2ebc6d520c1f9f94ee16e6ec5`
on the three roles **does not** return the applications to Wave 6 by

the backup above (execution `flotf2knv5rfajktkyivtcnk`) together with
that image tag, and loses what was written after 11:17:20.
